SidePiece

A slim Telegram panel that lives on the edge of your Mac screen. Hover the strip and your chats slide out; move away and it docks itself.

Unofficial. Not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Telegram.

Join the free beta

Free during beta. macOS 12 or later. Just send an email, no account needed.

What it does

1
Always one hover away
A thin strip pinned to the left or right screen edge. Rest your cursor on it for a beat and the panel slides out; it ignores drive-by brushes on the way to a scrollbar.
2
Keep Panel Open when you need it
Pin the panel open from the menu bar so it stays put while you type, then unpin it and it docks itself again.
3
Your Telegram, already set up
Runs Telegram Web in a persistent session. Log in once and it stays logged in across quits.
4
Unread counts everywhere
A red pill on the strip, a badge on the Dock icon, and a count in the menu bar, with optional native notifications.
5
Polite with your links
Links in chats open in your normal browser. The panel itself stays on web.telegram.org; nothing else loads inside it.
6
Menu bar controls
Dock side, panel width, compact mode, expand on notification, and an explicit Launch at Login toggle that is off until you turn it on.

On macOS Sequoia and later you open it anyway via System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway, and the beta guide walks you through exactly what to expect.

Name and store risks, up front

"Telegram" is someone else's trademark. This app never uses Telegram's logo, and the name only appears descriptively ("for Telegram"). Telegram could still ask for a rename; a rename before launch is cheap, a rename after launch is not.

Apple's App Store often rejects apps that are thin wrappers around a website (guideline 4.2, minimum functionality). SidePiece's native docking, badges, and notifications may or may not be enough, so the first channel is direct download from this page, with the App Store as a later option.
